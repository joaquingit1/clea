# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good faith, criticize ideas and code rather than people, and remember that the person on
the other side of an issue is giving their time for free. Harassment, personal attacks and discrimination of any kind
are not welcome here.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour in this project's spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) is not acceptable, report it to
the maintainers through GitHub's report option on the comment, or privately to
[@joaquingit1](https://github.com/joaquingit1). Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow
this Code of Conduct, and may ban, temporarily or permanently, a contributor for behaviour they find inappropriate,
threatening, offensive or harmful.
